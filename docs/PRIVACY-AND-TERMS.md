# GLOBALPULSE — Privacy and service-use notice (draft)

**Status:** Draft for owner review. This is product copy, not legal advice or a claim of legal compliance. Replace the contact placeholders and obtain a jurisdiction-specific review before public launch.

## What GLOBALPULSE does

GLOBALPULSE aggregates publicly available service-health and incident information from official provider status pages. It does not independently measure the availability of those providers and does not guarantee that an incident feed is complete or instantaneous.

## Account information

If you create an account, GLOBALPULSE uses the email address and profile name needed to authenticate your account and store your provider subscriptions. Authentication and account records are processed by Supabase.

## Operational information

The service stores provider source metadata, source incident identifiers, incident titles, provider-supplied status and impact fields, source links, timestamps, and ingestion-run outcomes. Provider data is public source material; GLOBALPULSE retains source attribution.

## Cookies and browser storage

Supabase authentication may use browser storage to maintain your signed-in session. The current application does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs. Hosting providers may maintain infrastructure logs under their own terms.

## Sharing

GLOBALPULSE does not sell account data. Account information is shared with service providers required to host, authenticate, and operate the application, including Supabase, Vercel, and Render, subject to their terms and privacy policies.

## Retention and deletion

Provider incident records are retained as operational history. Account profile and subscription data are retained while the account is active. A self-service account deletion workflow is not yet enabled; contact the operator to request account deletion and associated personal-data removal.

## Security

The application uses Supabase Auth, database Row Level Security, least-privilege browser keys, and server-side secrets. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected vulnerabilities responsibly.
